How these numbers are produced

A company counts only if the first cold email to its domain was sent before anyone from that company created a TimelinesAI workspace.

Domain matching alone is not enough — it credits the campaign for companies that had already found us, and for companies that have been customers for a year. Both were happening. Every row above is checked against the actual send timestamp from the SmartLead message history, not the date a lead was uploaded to a list.

  • Sends, replies, bounces, send timestamps — SmartLead API, all 43 campaigns created since 10 June, read per campaign.
  • Signups, seats, subscriptions, payments — TimelinesAI production database; MRR is the active Stripe subscription (seats × seat price), annual plans divided by twelve.
  • Demos — call recordings, matched to a campaign reply by the attendee.
  • Not counted here: LinkedIn outreach, which is a separate in-house motion, and any company whose signup predates its first email.
