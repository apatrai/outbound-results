How these numbers are produced

A company counts only if the first cold email to it was sent — actual SENT timestamp from the SmartLead message history — before the first person from that company registered.

Registration is measured at the person level in the production database (the user's join timestamp), not by workspace creation — a company can have an old workspace and a new person, and the two dates differ exactly in the cases that matter. Lead-upload dates are never used: uploading a lead is not sending an email.

  • Sends, bounces, send timestamps — SmartLead API, all 44 campaigns created since 10 June. Volumes are all-time per campaign (the API does not window them); replies, positives, signups, demos and payments are strictly 24 July – 7 August.
  • Positive replies — since 24 July SmartLead's automatic categorisation is switched off, so a reply is marked positive only when a human or our reply agent labels it. The 41 here are therefore hand-marked, not machine-guessed; the number is not directly comparable with earlier periods.
  • Signups, seats, payments, MRR — TimelinesAI production database; MRR is the active Stripe subscription (seats × seat price), annual plans divided by twelve. Every first payment in the window was swept, not only the ones we expected: 58 companies paid for the first time, three touched a lead list, two survived the checks.
  • Demos — Calendly bookings matched to campaign leads by invitee email (exact match or company domain), cross-checked against the booking notifications in the sales inbox so that bookings made now for later dates are not lost. Both sources returned the same set of companies this period.
  • Mailbox cross-check — every attributed company is searched across the sales team's mailboxes. Any company already in one-to-one conversation with us before it signed up is removed from the count; that check is what moved Sasa Consultoría out this period.
  • Excluded throughout: consumer email domains, bot and test workspaces, our own addresses, and the in-house LinkedIn motion.
