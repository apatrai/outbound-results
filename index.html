How these numbers are produced

A company counts only if the first cold email to it was sent — actual SENT timestamp from the SmartLead message history — before the first person from that company registered.

Registration is measured at the person level in the production database (the user's join timestamp), not by workspace creation — a company can have an old workspace and a new person, and the two dates differ exactly in the cases that matter. Lead-upload dates are never used: uploading a lead is not sending an email.

  • Sends, bounces, send timestamps — SmartLead API, all 43 campaigns created since 10 June. Volumes are all-time per campaign (the API does not window them); replies, positives, signups, demos and payments are strictly 9–24 July.
  • Positive replies — SmartLead reply categories (Interested / Meeting request / Information request); indicative, not hand-vetted one by one.
  • Signups, seats, payments, MRR — TimelinesAI production database; MRR is the active Stripe subscription (seats × seat price), annual plans divided by twelve.
  • Demos — Calendly bookings matched to campaign leads by invitee email (exact match or company domain), deduplicated per company, counted by meeting date; cancellations, support sessions and shared vendor domains excluded. Bookings made in the period for later dates are listed separately and cross-checked against the booking notifications in the sales inbox.
  • Excluded throughout: consumer email domains, bot and test workspaces, our own addresses, and the in-house LinkedIn motion.
